01 / THE TEAM WHO WE ARE

AEROSPACE, AEROMODELLING & UNMANNED SYSTEMS

Not just ideas.
Aircraft that fly.

AeroBMSCE is the aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems club of BMS College of Engineering. Established in 2012 and operating from Propel Lab–3, we bring together students from 10+ departments to design, build and test aircraft, drones and autonomous systems.

From competitive aircraft design to drone racing and fabrication, our work is hands-on by design: concepts are modelled, hardware is integrated, and ideas are put to the test.
